Refresh logs & PDF backup (Google Drive)

Each time you press Refresh on a project, the dashboard makes a PDF of that project’s reports (KPIs, S-curve, cost & units summary, project detail, delayed activities), saves it in your Google Drive and writes a line in the Refresh Logs tab. It needs a small Google Apps Script (file RefreshLogs_AppsScript.gs) deployed once from your Google account — paste its web app URL and key below.

Settings & help

If a project says its data could not be loaded, check these in order:
  • The sheet must be shared as Anyone with the link — Viewer. A sheet limited to your company or to named people cannot be read by the browser.
  • The two tab names must match exactly: S-Curve and Progress Data (spelling, spaces and capitals).
  • Progress Data can be the raw P6 resource-assignment export: P6 codes in row 1 and names in row 2 are both recognised.
  • The device needs internet access to docs.google.com.
  • If the sheet was just shared, wait a minute and press Refresh — Google caches the permission for a short while.
How the numbers are worked out
  • Planned cost = BCWS, earned value = BCWP, actual cost = ACWP, all summed from the resource assignment rows.
  • Saving = earned value − actual cost. Saved units = earned units − actual units.
  • SPI = earned ÷ planned. CPI = earned ÷ actual. Budget health reads under budget when CPI is 1.00 or better.
  • Progress % = planned or earned value as a share of total budgeted cost.
  • Plumbing and finishing labour resources carry no unit of measure and are left out of the category table, matching the Power BI report.
